Educational Boundary Notice

VehiCare is an educational pre-workshop triage assistant designed to prepare owners for service conversations. It does not replace physical inspections by certified technicians or specialized diagnostic scan tools.

VehiCare Diagnostic Guidance (Confidence: 92% • Est. Cost: ₱450–₱1,850 PHP):

  • Urgency: Immediate inspection needed for rear hydraulic caliper; Routine adjustment for valve lash at next periodic service.
  • Recommended Oil Spec: 10W-40 4T JASO MA2 (wet-clutch shear and friction protection).

Multi-Vehicle Engineering

Engineered for two wheels and four.

A motorcycle drive chain requires fundamentally different care than an automotive OBD-II powertrain or a bicycle hydraulic disc brake.

Two-Wheeled Powertrain

Motorcycles & Scooters

High-RPM single and twin cylinders, wet clutches, and exposed final drive chains.

  • Drive Chain Slack: 25–35 mm tension checks, cleaning, and lubrication every 500 km or after heavy rain.
  • 4T Engine Oil: 1,500–3,000 km intervals with JASO MA2 motorcycle oil for wet-clutch shear protection.
  • Brake Friction Material: Pad thickness inspections against backing plates to prevent gouging disc rotors.
Sample: Yamaha Sniper 150 • Vespa Sprint 150
Passenger Vehicles

Cars, Sedans & SUVs

OBD-II emission diagnostics, 12V charging systems, and hydraulic brake lines.

  • OBD-II Fault Codes: Check Engine light interpretation (e.g. P0420 catalytic efficiency) and freeze frames.
  • 12V Battery Health: Parasitic drain isolation (>50 mA), terminal sulfation, and alternator charging rates.
  • Fluids & Alignment: Brake master cylinder bypass checks, coolant reservoir levels, and tire wear patterns.
Sample: Honda Civic 1.5 Turbo • Toyota Corolla Altis
Pedal & e-Bike

Bicycles & MTBs

Hydraulic mineral oil calipers, multi-speed cassettes, and suspension linkage.

  • Hydraulic Disc Bleeds: Mineral oil syringe bleeding for spongy levers and isopropyl pad deglazing.
  • Chain Elongation: 0.5%–0.75% stretch gauge verification to protect cassette teeth from sharking.
  • Drivetrain Indexing: Barrel adjuster tension and derailleur hanger alignment for crisp, non-skipping shifts.
Sample: Trek Marlin 7 • Gravel / Commuter

Technical FAQ

Clear answers about VehiCare.

Details on diagnostic scope, safety boundaries, and application release architecture.

Is VehiCare AI a replacement for a qualified mechanic? +

No. VehiCare is an educational pre-workshop triage assistant designed to help owners understand symptoms, assess urgency, and plan repair costs.

For safety-critical concerns such as brake failure, fuel leaks, suspension collapse, or severe electrical shorts, always seek physical inspection from a certified mechanic.

Which vehicle types are supported in VehiCare? +

VehiCare organizes vehicles using a normalized 4-tier database catalog (Category → Brand → Model → Year → Variant) across three major classifications:

  • Motorcycles & Scooters: Tailored for single and twin cylinders (e.g., Yamaha Sniper 150, Vespa Sprint 150, Honda Click 125i) with wet-clutch 4T JASO MA2 oil thresholds and 25–35 mm chain tension tracking.
  • Passenger Cars & SUVs: Tailored for OBD-II emission fault codes (Check Engine codes), 12V battery sulfation/parasitic drain, and hydraulic master cylinder maintenance (e.g., Honda Civic 1.5T, Toyota Corolla Altis).
  • Bicycles & MTBs: Tailored for hydraulic mineral oil bleeds, 0.5%–0.75% chain elongation stretch limits, and rear derailleur indexing (e.g., Trek Marlin 7, Specialized Rockhopper).
How does multimodal concern submission work in the app? +

VehiCare's dedicated Node.js AI microservice, powered by Google Gemini 3.8 Flash, evaluates 4 distinct input modalities:

  • Text: Natural automotive language input with bilingual English and Filipino / Taglish conversational parsing.
  • Acoustic Audio: Sound clip recording evaluated by our dedicated vehicle sound validation service to identify mechanical knocks, valve clicks, and bearing whines while ignoring speech and street noise.
  • High-Res Photos: Inspection of brake friction pads, tire tread wear, and fluid leaks, paired with visual vehicle category mismatch verification.
  • Video Clips: Up to 30-second clips for observing dynamic motion, belt slip, and exhaust smoke discoloration over time.
What is the difference between Free and Premium plans? +

The Free Plan (₱0 free forever) includes 1 active vehicle profile with standard AI symptom triage, urgent health score monitoring, and nearby repair shop discovery.

The Premium Plan (₱149 / month or ₱1,490 / year) expands garage capacity to 5 protected vehicle profiles with instant context switching, acoustic audio knock analysis, camera part wear detection, video diagnostics, and searchable activity history with AI summaries.
