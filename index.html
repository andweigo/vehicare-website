Multi-Vehicle Coverage

Engineered for two wheels and four.

A motorcycle drive chain requires fundamentally different care than an automotive OBD-II powertrain or a bicycle hydraulic disc brake.

Two-Wheeled Powertrain

Motorcycles & Scooters

•
Drive Chain Slack: 20–30mm tension checks, cleaning, and lubrication every 500 km.
•
4T Motorcycle Oil: Strict 1,000–1,500 km intervals due to shared wet clutch shear wear.
•
Brake Friction Material: Pad thickness inspection against backing plate to prevent rotor disc damage.
Sample Profile: Yamaha Sniper 150 • Vespa Sprint 150
Passenger Vehicles

Cars, Sedans & SUVs

•
OBD-II Codes: Check Engine Light (CEL) fault interpretation and sensor freeze frames.
•
12V Battery Health: Parasitic drain detection, terminal sulfation, and alternator charging rates.
•
Fluids & Alignment: Coolant levels, transmission fluid, and tire camber/toe wear patterns.
Sample Profile: Honda Civic 1.5 Turbo • Toyota Corolla
Pedal & E-Bike

Bicycles & MTBs

•
Hydraulic Disc Brakes: Mineral oil bleeding for spongy lever stroke and pad deglazing.
•
Chain Elongation: 0.5% – 0.75% stretch gauge verification to protect cassette cogs.
•
Drivetrain Indexing: Barrel adjuster tuning for crisp, non-skipping gear shifts.
Sample Profile: Trek Marlin 7 • Gravel / Commuter

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about VehiCare.

Details on diagnostic scope, safety boundaries, and application release architecture.

Is VehiCare AI a replacement for a qualified mechanic? +

No. VehiCare is an educational pre-workshop triage assistant designed to help owners understand symptoms, assess urgency, and plan repair costs.

For safety-critical concerns such as brake failure, fuel leaks, suspension collapse, or severe electrical shorts, always seek physical inspection from a certified mechanic.

Which vehicle types are supported? +

VehiCare supports three vehicle categories: passenger cars & SUVs, motorcycles & scooters, and bicycles & e-bikes, with distinct service thresholds for each.

How does multimodal concern submission work in the app? +

In the Android application, you can describe issues via text, record mechanical sounds (engine knocking, belt squeals) via microphone, or take photos of worn components (tire tread, brake pads).

What is the difference between Free and Premium plans? +

The Free Plan includes 1 active vehicle profile with standard AI symptom triage. The Premium Plan (shown in our app screenshots with 5/5 profiles) unlocks up to 5 protected profiles, multimodal audio/photo submissions, and deep activity history.

How do I configure the real APK download URL in this website? +

Open script.js and configure APP_CONFIG.apkDownloadUrl with your GitHub Release or hosted APK link. Once set, all download buttons will fetch the APK immediately.
